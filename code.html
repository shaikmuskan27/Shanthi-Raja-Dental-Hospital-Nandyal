COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance plans does Shanthi Raja Dental accept? expand_more
We accept all major corporate health insurances, cashless TPA policies, UPI, credit/debit cards, and offer 0% EMI financing options for orthodontic aligners, braces, and implant treatments.
Are you accepting new patients in Nandyal, AP? expand_more
Yes! We warmly welcome new patients of all ages across Nandyal, Allagadda, Banaganapalle, and surrounding areas. Walk-ins and pre-booked consultations are available 7 days a week.
Do you offer evening or weekend dental appointments? expand_more
Yes, our clinic is open Monday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, including late evening slots so you can receive quality specialist dental care after work or school.
Are root canal treatments painful at Shanthi Raja Dental? expand_more
Not at all. Under the care of our endodontist Dr. Balusa Rajeev (M.D.S.), we utilize modern computer-assisted local anesthesia, rotary endodontics, and apex locators for gentle, virtually painless single-sitting root canals.
Where is the clinic located in Nandyal? expand_more
We are located at Doctors Complex, directly opposite HP Petrol Bunk on the main corridor in Priyanka Nagar, Farooq Nagar, Nandyal, AP 518501.
