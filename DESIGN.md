## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, hospitality, and clinical excellence of a premium dental boutique. It blends the serene ambience of high-end wellness spas with the authoritative trust of medical precision. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a refined **Modern Editorial Minimalism** mixed with **Soft Tactile Glassmorphism**. Clean off-white canvases allow photographic portraits, patient testimonials, and pristine clinical spaces to breathe. Typography pairs a high-contrast editorial serif for headlines with an ultra-clean, legible grotesque sans-serif for functional UI and copy. The emotional tone avoids cold, sterile clinical cliches in favor of comforting, reassuring, and distinguished healthcare design.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate atmosphere of confidence and tranquility:

- **Primary (`#0b1e36`)**: A rich deep midnight navy providing authoritative weight, utilized for main headers, deep footer sections, primary text, and high-trust anchors.
- **Secondary (`#48a964` / `#52b788`)**: A restorative botanical green engineered specifically for conversion touchpoints, including primary appointment CTAs, success feedback, and trust highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#1e3a8a`)**: Saturated clinical royal blue used for subtle category tags, link highlights, and secondary interactive moments.
- **Neutral (`#f8fafc` / `#fcfdfe`)**: Ultra-clean, warm spa off-white establishing an airy, calming environment that reduces patient anxiety while maintaining spotless clarity.
- **Slate & Supporting Neutrals (`#334155`, `#64748b`)**: Calibrated slate hues providing soft secondary contrast for body paragraphs, subtitles, and border separators.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes an intentional balance between high-end editorial prestige and medical accessibility:

- **Playfair Display**: Assigned to display and headline tiers. Headings frequently feature italicized accent phrases (e.g. *"meets genuine warmth"*) to introduce human warmth and relaxed sophistication into clinical titles.
- **Inter**: Serves as the high-legibility workhorse across body copy, navigational anchors, metric callouts, and interactive UI. Its neutral geometry balances the serif headers.
- **Label Caps**: Used for eyebrow badges (e.g., "WELCOME TO PREMIER ARTS DENTAL") to structure page scanning with crisp vertical cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1280px, flanked by generous section padding to foster a relaxed, unhurried reading experience:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, 48px outer margins. Section blocks employ spacious 64px to 96px vertical margins (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Multi-column cards shift to 2-column stacked configurations.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins. Banners and imagery collapse into seamless single-column flows, while action bars remain sticky or thumb-friendly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines soft architectural layering with translucent glassmorphic components:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: The base tone `#fcfdfe` hosts subtle linear gradients or pale sky-blue radial glows to mimic sunlight pouring through clinic windows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces)**: Pure white `#ffffff` cards supported by a faint border (`rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.06)`) and a diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating & Interactive Elevation)**: Floating navigation headers, hover cards, and doctor spotlight panels utilize frosted translucent glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);`) with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Booking Flyouts)**: Elevated interactive elements cast deep, soft shadows (`0 24px 48px -8px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.16)`) ensuring absolute clinical focus.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous curvature to evoke softness, comfort, and approachable luxury:

- **Containers & Feature Cards**: Feature `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.5rem) corners, softening medical photography and framing doctor profiles.
- **Hero Image Framing**: Employs large `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem to 2rem) radii on photography grids, echoing the organic shape language of contemporary architectural clinic design.
- **Buttons & Pills**: Primary conversion buttons and category badges adopt full pill radii (`rounded-full`), reinforcing friendliness and inviting immediate physical interaction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Appointment CTA**: Full pill shape with `#48a964` background, white text (`Inter 15px Semibold`), and subtle inset highlight. On hover, transitions to `#3d9154` with a gentle upward lift of 1px and a tinted soft green halo.
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent dark navy surface (`rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.06)`) or ghost border (`1.5px solid #0b1e36`), transitioning to solid navy on interaction.
- **Phone / Quick Connect**: Rounded pill with subtle border and embedded phone/calendar SVG glyphs spaced by `space-sm`.

### Chips & High-Trust Badges
- **Review & Rating Chips**: Frosted glass badges containing 5 golden stars, a numerical score (`4.9`), and micro-copy ("Trusted by Freehold families") in `label-md`.
- **Insurance Badges**: Clean white pill cards bounded by `1px solid rgba(15, 31, 56, 0.08)` featuring verified insurer marks with crisp muted typography.

### Cards & Service Containers
- **Treatment & Service Cards**: Pure white card with 24px internal padding, `rounded-2xl` corners, subtle 1px border, and an icon tinted in `#eef8f2` containing green or deep blue stroke icons.
- **Doctor / Staff Spotlight**: Split or framed card with large rounded photography, warm biographical excerpts, and high-contrast Playfair headers.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: 48px height, rounded-xl borders in `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.2)`, utilizing Inter 15px with `#0b1e36` active text. Focus state triggers a 2px highlight in `#48a964` with no harsh outline.
- **Date / Time Pickers**: Clean calendar grid surfaces with rounded-full selection indicators finished in secondary medical green.
